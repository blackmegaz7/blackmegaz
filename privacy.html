Privacy Policy

MiRclone is a personal OAuth client operated by a single individual (the owner) to access the owner's own Google Drive files with rclone.

  • It accesses only the owner's own Google Drive account.
  • Data stays on the owner's own devices. It is not collected by any server, shared with third parties, sold, or used to train AI models.
  • The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
